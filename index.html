Congressional Deep Research

A multi-agent research system investigating campaign contributions, donor affiliations, and FY2027 earmark requests.

About This Research Agent

What it explores: Congressional campaign contributions, political action committee (PAC) donors, donor affiliations, and FY2027 earmark requests. Multiple research agents investigate the available data and compile findings into a research report.

Who it's for: Students, journalists, researchers, and anyone interested in congressional funding and campaign finance transparency.

How to use it: Enter a question about a member of Congress, or select an example below to fill in the question box. Click Run Research to start the investigation. Agent activity appears as the system works, followed by a completed research report.
